# Account and Privacy

## AC-1 · Deleting a PW account
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/account-deletion

Account deletion is requested in the PW app: open the app, tap the menu at the top left, go to Profile, open Settings, choose Additional settings, then Account Deletion. After deletion, using PW again needs a new account.

## AC-2 · What PW keeps after deletion
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/account-deletion and https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

PW is required by law to keep certain data after an account is deleted, such as order history, for tax and security reasons. Content a student posted publicly (for example in discussions) and their name on it may remain visible.

## AC-3 · Stopping marketing messages
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

Students can stop promotional messages by using the unsubscribe link in PW emails, changing their communication preferences, or emailing support@pw.live to block promotional communication. PW states it does not sell or rent personal information to third parties for their marketing without explicit consent.

## AC-4 · Keeping the account secure
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/terms-and-conditions and https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/

An account is for one student and must not be shared. Students should log out at the end of each session and tell PW immediately about any unauthorised use of their account. PW can refuse access or terminate accounts that break its terms.

## AC-5 · Changing the registered mobile number, email or name
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

The registered mobile number is the login and is linked to all purchases, so it is changed only by the support team after verifying the student's identity and order details. Name and email can usually be edited in Profile; if not, support can update them.

## AC-6 · Data and privacy concerns, and the Grievance Officer
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/contact-us and https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

PW processes student data in India under Indian data-protection rules; for minors, consent is given by parents or guardians at registration. Complaints about how personal information is processed can be sent to PW's Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance.officer@pw.live (phone +91-7406346660 / +91-8448982616; Noida One, 1st Floor, Industrial Area, Sector 62, Noida, UP 201309), with the subject line "Attention: Grievance Redressal Officer".

## AC-7 · Use of names and photos in PW promotions
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

PW's terms allow it to use a student's name, photo, rank and testimonials in its promotions. A student or parent who objects should write to support@pw.live; the request is reviewed by the support team.
