# Batch Access

## BA-1 · Purchased batch not showing in the app
Source: assumed for this prototype; the My Batches path is official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

A newly bought batch can take up to 2 hours to appear. First, log out and log back in with the same mobile number used for payment, then open Study > My Batches. If the batch is still missing after 2 hours, the support team has to check the order manually: share the order ID or payment screenshot and the registered mobile number.

## BA-2 · Batch validity and expiry
Source: assumed for this prototype; validity per batch is shown on PW batch pages (e.g. "Valid until NEET 2027") — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Each batch has a validity shown on its batch page, usually tied to the target exam (for example "valid until NEET 2027"). Lectures, notes and tests stay available until then. After expiry the content can no longer be opened. Validity is not extended automatically; extension requests are reviewed by the support team.

## BA-3 · Logging in on more than one device
Source: public reports of PW app behaviour — https://www.primebook.in/blog/how-to-use-physics-wallah-app-on-two-devices ; the rule against sharing accounts is official — https://store.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

The PW app can be used on more than one device, but not at the same time: logging in on a second device logs the account out of the first. Accounts are for one student and must not be shared; sharing or unusual activity can lead to the account being locked. A locked or blocked account can only be restored by the support team after verifying the registered mobile number and order details.

## BA-4 · Finding lectures, notes and DPPs
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Open the PW app, go to Study > My Batches and select the batch. Choose the subject and chapter; each lecture lists its class notes and DPPs (daily practice problems). Live classes appear on the batch schedule and recordings are added after the class. Announcements and demo lectures are in the batch's notice section.

## BA-5 · Free content
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/faqs and https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.penpencil.physicswala

Notes for all classes are free on the PW website and will stay free. Free lectures and resources are also in the app's library section and on the Physics Wallah YouTube channel. A student does not need to buy a batch to use free content.

## BA-6 · Power Batch
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/power-batch

Power Batch is PW's small-group programme with 1:1 teacher interaction, dual-teacher support for instant doubt solving, and dedicated support for non-academic issues. Students can book a free Power class before joining. Power Batch has its own counsellor line: 07290070560.

## BA-7 · Test series access (PW Test Pass)
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/test-series

PW Test Series are sold as Test Passes (for example 12-month JEE Test Passes). Tests and their solutions are available in Hindi and English, can be attempted in the PW app or on the mobile website, and come with detailed analysis and state and all-India ranks. Some passes include offline computer-based tests at PW centres and, for Pro passes, home delivery of test papers.
