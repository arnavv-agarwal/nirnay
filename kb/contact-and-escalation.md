# Contact and Escalation

## CT-1 · PW support channels
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/contact-us

PW support is available 24x7 on 07406346660 and 08448982616, and by email at support@pw.live. If the line is busy, students are asked to call again after 15 minutes. PW aims to resolve queries within 7 days. Inside the PW app, the "Contact us" option is the fastest way to raise an issue.

## CT-2 · Talking to a counsellor
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/faqs, https://www.pw.live/contact-us and https://www.pw.live/power-batch

For help choosing a course, students can give a missed call to 07019243492 or call 07406346660 / 08448982616. Power Batch counselling: 07290070560. Vidyapeeth admissions helpdesk: +91 9513350005. PW Skills sales: 09513883111.

## CT-3 · When a support agent takes over
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

A support agent personally handles any request that needs a change to the student's account, payment, order or batch (refunds, batch changes, missing batches after payment, blocked accounts, invoice corrections), every complaint about a counsellor or mis-selling, and any message where the student is very upset or in distress.

## CT-4 · Students in distress
Source: assumed for this prototype; Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's mental-health helpline — https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/

If a student says they feel hopeless, extremely stressed or unsafe, a support agent reaches out personally and as a priority. Tele-MANAS, the Government of India's free mental-health helpline, is available 24x7 at 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416). In an emergency, call 112.

## CT-5 · Escalating a complaint
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/contact-us and https://www.pw.live/contact-us

If a complaint isn't resolved through support, students can write to support@pw.live with their ticket details. Grievances about personal data go to the Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance.officer@pw.live (see AC-6).
