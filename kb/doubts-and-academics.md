# Doubts and Academics

## AD-1 · Asking a subject doubt
Source: public PW product info — https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/microsoft-research-and-physics-wallah-team-up-to-enhance-ai-based-tutoring/ and https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.penpencil.physicswala

Subject doubts (physics, chemistry, maths, biology and others) are answered fastest by AI Guru, the AI help tool in the PW app. Students can type the question, speak it, or upload a photo of the problem, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. The support team does not solve subject questions.

## AD-2 · Doubts during a live class
Source: public PW product info — https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/microsoft-research-and-physics-wallah-team-up-to-enhance-ai-based-tutoring/

During a live class, students can post doubts through the doubt option in the class. The Smart Doubt Engine answers them instantly, with text or short video explanations.

## AD-3 · When the AI answer didn't help
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

If an AI answer is unclear or seems wrong, the student can mark it as unhelpful and ask again with more detail, or take it to their batch's doubt support. Batches with mentorship or doubt sessions have teachers who can review the doubt.

## AD-4 · Study plans and schedules
Source: public PW product info — https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/physics-wallah-unveils-indias-first-indigenously-built-ai-education-suite-alakh-ai-records-1-5-million-users-in-less-than-2-months/

Study Sahayak in the app builds a personalised study plan from the student's subjects, chapters and goals. The batch schedule shows upcoming live classes and tests.

## AD-5 · In-person and 1:1 doubt support
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/power-batch and https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Power Batch students get dual-teacher support for instant doubt solving and 1:1 teacher interaction. Vidyapeeth offline students get in-person doubt help from teachers at their centre (up to 8 hours for the batch shown on PW's site), and Pathshala centres have a doubt-clearing faculty in the classroom.

## AD-6 · Exam registration, admit cards and results
Source: official — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.penpencil.physicswala

PW is an independent educational platform, not affiliated with any government body or exam authority. PW does not handle exam registration, admit cards, results or verification; students must use the official exam websites (for example NTA for JEE/NEET, upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, cbse.gov.in). PW's mock tests, PYQs and practice questions are created by PW and are not official exam papers.

## AD-7 · Test series results and ranks
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/test-series

After each PW test, students get a detailed performance report with state and all-India ranks, and solutions in Hindi and English. If a result or rank is missing, the support team can check the attempt (see TS-5).
