# Offline Centres: PW Vidyapeeth and Pathshala

## OC-1 · Vidyapeeth vs Pathshala
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/contact-us and https://www.pw.live/pathshala-center-state-list

PW runs offline learning in two formats. Vidyapeeth is a fully offline classroom. Pathshala is hybrid: students learn from online lectures with a doubt-clearing faculty at the centre. Some centres follow a 2-teacher model, with one teacher explaining and one helping with doubts. Vidyapeeth offers JEE and NEET batches (Class 11 over 2 years, Class 12 and droppers over 1 year) and foundation batches for Classes 8–10.

## OC-2 · Booking a seat online
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

A seat at a Vidyapeeth or Pathshala centre can be booked online on the PW app or website for ₹5,000. This seat-booking amount is non-refundable, and it is adjusted into the final total fee when the student visits the centre.

## OC-3 · Admission steps and documents
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

After registering, fill in the admission form sent by SMS, open the Student Portal, then visit the centre to complete pending formalities and fee payment. Documents are usually not needed for a regular visit, but may be needed for scholarships; keep digital copies of Aadhaar (or other ID), the Class 10 marksheet if applicable, and bank details. Vidyapeeth helpdesk: +91 9513350005.

## OC-4 · Batch allotment, ID card and study material
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

The batch is allotted after the first instalment is paid and the ERP admission form is completed, based on preference and seat availability; PW can change allotments. The ID card and welcome kit are collected at the centre after allotment, and a valid ID card is required to enter. Study materials are given after the ID card; DPPs are available at the centre after each class.

## OC-5 · Vidyapeeth refund policy
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Refunds follow the PW Vidyapeeth Refund Policy. Refund requests must be raised in person at the centre; requests made verbally or by phone, email or fax are not accepted. The registration fee, book/module fee, doubt-series fee, test-series fee and the PWNSAT fee are non-refundable in all cases.

## OC-6 · Security deposit
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

A ₹1,000 security deposit is paid with the admission form to cover use of the premises. It is refunded, after deducting outstanding dues and any verified damage claims, only when the student withdraws or completes the course.

## OC-7 · Transferring between centres
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/contact-us

A student cannot transfer between Pathshala and Vidyapeeth, in either direction. Transfers from one Vidyapeeth centre to another (for example Kota to Delhi) are possible by talking to the counsellor of the centre concerned.

## OC-8 · App access and recordings for offline students
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Offline students get access to their batch on the PW app within 24–48 hours of batch allotment. Batch details are under Study > My Batches. Recorded lectures, video solutions and test analysis are on the PW app; the batch's notice section shows how to find them.
