# Payments

## PY-1 · Payment methods
Source: assumed for this prototype; PW Skills' payment-gateway statement is official — https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/

Batches can be paid for with UPI, debit or credit card, and net banking, through the payment gateway in the PW app or on pw.live. PW does not store card details; payments are processed by partner payment gateways. Students should pay only inside the official app or website, never to a personal account or an unknown UPI ID.

## PY-2 · Money deducted but payment failed
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

If money was deducted but the app shows the payment as failed, the bank normally reverses the amount automatically within 5–7 working days, and no action is needed. If the money was deducted, the payment shows as successful, and the batch is still not added after 2 hours, the support team must check the transaction: share the transaction ID (UTR) and the registered mobile number.

## PY-3 · Invoices and receipts
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

The invoice for each purchase can be downloaded from the orders section of the app or website. If the invoice needs a correction (name, GST details), the support team raises it.

## PY-4 · Coupons and discounts
Source: assumed for this prototype; coupon use at checkout is widely documented

Coupons must be applied on the payment page before paying ("Apply coupon"). A coupon cannot be added to an order that is already paid. Each coupon has its own validity and eligible batches.

## PY-5 · EMI and education loans
Source: public news reports — https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-physicswallah-student-loan-business-repeat-byju-debt-trap/ and https://www.newsdrum.in/business/physicswallah-to-exit-student-financing-biz-ties-up-with-nbfcs-instead-12000448

For higher-fee courses (especially offline Vidyapeeth and hybrid programmes) PW offers split payments and EMIs, and education loans through lending partners (NBFCs). A loan is not the same as a no-cost EMI: it can carry interest and a processing fee. Students and parents should read the loan terms (interest rate, fees, tenure, lender name) before signing, and can ask support to confirm whether a plan is a loan.

## PY-6 · Price changed before paying
Source: official (PW Store) — https://store.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

Prices shown in the cart are the latest prices; adding an item to the cart does not reserve its price, and the price can go up or down before the order is placed.

## PY-7 · Fake payment requests in PW's name
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/ (PW will not ask for your password) ; guidance assumed for this prototype

PW never asks students for OTPs, passwords, card PINs, or payment to personal accounts over calls or WhatsApp. Anyone asking for these is not PW. Students should not pay or share the OTP, and should report the number or message to support@pw.live.
