# PW OnlyIAS (UPSC)

## UP-1 · Refund policy for PW OnlyIAS offline centres
Source: official — https://pwonlyias.com/refund-and-cancellation-policy/

The seat booking (registration) amount of ₹11,800 is non-refundable in all cases; registration, infrastructure and study-material fees are also non-refundable. Within 30 days of the batch start date a student is eligible for a full refund of the tuition fee (the seat booking amount is deducted); after 30 days there is no refund. Refund requests must be made in writing to info@pwonlyias.com and can take up to 15 business days. Extra payments made by mistake are refunded after verification, minus any administrative charges.

## UP-2 · PW OnlyIAS modes and centres
Source: official — https://pwonlyias.com/refund-and-cancellation-policy/

PW OnlyIAS offers UPSC and State PSC preparation online, offline and in hybrid mode, with offline centres including Delhi, Lucknow and Patna. Counsellors help choose a course and centre.
