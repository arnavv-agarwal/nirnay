# PW Skills (career courses)

## SK-1 · PW Skills support
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/ and https://pwskills.com/contact-us/

PW Skills (pwskills.com) has its own support: email support@pwskills.com or raise a ticket from the course dashboard (LMS > Contact Us). Queries are attended to within 24 business hours, and support usually calls or emails within 7–8 hours. Sales and offline-course counselling: 09513883111. PW Skills courses are on pwskills.com only; there is no PW Skills app.

## SK-2 · PW Skills refund policy
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/ (Refund Policy section)

Refunds depend on the course type. **Basic** (self-paced, recorded) courses are non-refundable. **Premium** and **Pro** (live + hybrid) courses can be refunded if the request is raised within **30 calendar days** of purchase, minus a non-refundable registration fee of ₹10,000 (₹5,000 for Banking & Finance courses). After 30 days there is no refund, whatever the reason or course usage, and course access is revoked when a refund is processed. The same rules apply to full payment, part payment, EMI, loan and FlexiPay purchases. (PW Skills' older FAQ still says "strict no-refund policy"; the detailed terms above are the current policy.)

## SK-3 · How a PW Skills refund is processed
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/

Refund requests go to support@pwskills.com or LMS > Contact Us > Raise a support request, within the 30-day window; keep the ticket number. The team first tries to resolve the concern (days 1–4); if the learner still wants a refund, it is initiated on day 5 and usually credited within 7–10 working days. For EMI, loan or FlexiPay purchases, PW Skills starts the cancellation with the financing partner, whose own process and timelines apply.

## SK-4 · Enrolled but no course access
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/

The course dashboard should be available right after enrolling. If it isn't, contact support@pwskills.com with the registered email and payment details.

## SK-5 · Certificates
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/ and https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/

The course completion certificate is generated from the course analytics section of the dashboard once the course's requirements (videos, assignments, etc.) are met. PW Skills certificates are not recognised by regulators such as AICTE or UGC, and some programmes don't offer a certificate.

## SK-6 · Missed live classes, recordings and assignments
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/

Live sessions are recorded and uploaded to the dashboard within 24 working hours. Lectures can't be downloaded; they stay available on the dashboard. Assignments submitted late are not evaluated, except in genuine cases (health issues, family emergencies) reviewed by the team. If a submitted assignment isn't updated, contact support@pwskills.com.

## SK-7 · Pausing a programme
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/terms-and-conditions/

Learners in eligible new programmes can pause for up to 3 months in total. During a pause, recordings and doubt resolution stay available but no new lectures are added. Pausing beyond 3 months is chargeable and needs PW Skills' approval. Enrolment in a programme can't be transferred to another person.

## SK-8 · Job assistance
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/

Job assistance means PW Skills brings interview opportunities to learners after they successfully complete an eligible programme and meet its criteria; the specific terms are on each programme's page. It is not a job guarantee.

## SK-9 · Doubts in PW Skills courses
Source: official — https://pwskills.com/faqs/

Whether a PW Skills programme includes doubt support is listed in the programme details. Where it does, learners clear doubts in the doubt-clearing sessions held on set days for their course, with subject experts. PW Skills doubts are not handled by the PW app's AI Guru.
