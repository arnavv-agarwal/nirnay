# PW Store: Books and Orders

## ST-1 · Dispatch and delivery time
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/shipping-policy

Orders are shipped the next working day and are mostly delivered within 3–5 working days of dispatch (major cities). Deliveries to remote areas, or during strikes, bad weather, natural disasters or government action, can take up to 15–20 days. PW ships all week except public holidays, through partners such as Shiprocket, Shyplite, Delhivery, Xpressbees and India Post.

## ST-2 · Delivery charges
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/shipping-policy

Delivery charges depend on the cart value. Shipping is currently free for orders above ₹500.

## ST-3 · Order updates
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/shipping-policy

Students get SMS and WhatsApp updates when the order is placed, assigned to a courier, picked up, delivered, returned or refunded. If an order is not delivered 5 days, and again 10 days, after dispatch, they are told the reason and a new expected delivery date.

## ST-4 · Order not delivered
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/shipping-policy

If an order is not delivered 15–20 days after dispatch, PW initiates a refund for it. Before that, the student can track the order under My Orders or contact the courier directly (ST-8).

## ST-5 · Damaged, missing or wrong items
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/shipping-policy and https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

If a product arrives damaged, the student can fill in the "Return and Replace" form (the damaged item is picked up and a new one sent) or the "Return and Refund" form (refund once the item is picked up); both apply to selected products. If the courier damaged or lost the product, the student is entitled to a replacement. Share the order ID and photos of the damage.

## ST-6 · Cancelling an order
Source: official — https://store.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

An order can be cancelled at no charge before PW sends the shipment confirmation. Once shipped, the return and refund rules apply instead. PW can also cancel an order before shipping, for example if an item is out of stock.

## ST-7 · Wrong address or refused delivery
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

If the student gave a wrong address or refused the delivery, the order is not entitled to any refund. Donations to PW Foundation made with an order are non-refundable.
