# Refunds and Cancellations (online batches)

## RF-1 · Refund policy for online batches
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

Purchases of PW products and services are not entitled to a refund. Any online batch or service, once bought, is non-refundable. This applies whatever the reason: a change of mind, not liking the batch, or stopping studies. The exceptions are a batch bought by mistake (RF-2), payment errors (RF-3), and book orders damaged or lost in delivery (see the PW Store articles).

## RF-2 · Changing to a different batch
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions and https://www.pw.live/faqs

If a student bought a batch by mistake, they can ask to change it to another batch **of the same amount within 10 days of purchase**. Some batches have their own transfer window: for example, Lakshya batch students can move to Lakshya JEE 2.0 or Lakshya NEET 2.0 within 15 days. After the window closes the batch cannot be changed. A batch change is done by the support team, not automatically, so the student should share the order ID, the batch they bought and the batch they want.

## RF-3 · Charged twice, or charged by mistake
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

If the same order was paid for twice, the extra payment is returned to the original payment method once the support team has verified both transactions. This usually takes 5–7 working days after verification, depending on the bank. The student should share both transaction IDs (UTR/UPI reference numbers) or payment screenshots and the registered mobile number.

## RF-4 · How refund and batch-change requests are handled
Source: assumed for this prototype; the 7-day resolution target is official — https://www.pw.live/contact-us

Every refund, duplicate-charge or batch-change request is reviewed by a support agent, because it changes the student's payment or batch records; it is never decided automatically. The agent checks the order against the policy and replies with a decision. PW aims to resolve support queries within 7 days.

## RF-5 · Refund questions before buying
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/terms-and-conditions

Students and parents asking about refunds before buying should know that online batches are non-refundable, and that PW recommends checking the batch details (syllabus, schedule, faculty, validity) and the free content before paying. A mistaken purchase can only be switched to a batch of the same amount within 10 days (RF-2).

## RF-6 · A counsellor promised something different
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

If a student says a counsellor or sales call promised something the batch doesn't deliver (a different language, a guaranteed result, a different price or refund), a support agent reviews the complaint personally. The student should share the counsellor's name or number, the date of the call, and any messages or screenshots. The agent checks the sale and responds; the outcome is not decided automatically.

## RF-7 · Other PW programmes have their own refund rules
Source: official — see the linked policies

Refund rules differ by programme: PW Vidyapeeth offline centres (OC-5), PW Skills courses (SK-2), PW OnlyIAS offline centres (UP-1), and PW Store book orders (ST-4, ST-5). Always check which programme the student bought before quoting a policy.
