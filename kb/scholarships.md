# Scholarships (PWNSAT)

## SC-1 · What PWNSAT is
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/vp-blogs/exams/what-is-pwnsat

PWNSAT (Physics Wallah National Scholarship cum Admission Test) is PW's scholarship and admission test for Classes 5 to 12 and droppers. It can be taken online or offline. Students can win up to 100% scholarships, cash rewards and learning opportunities; the top 500 students can get free education and scholarship opportunities.

## SC-2 · PWNSAT 2026-27 dates and levels
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/vp-blogs/exams/what-is-pwnsat

Level 1 runs online every day from 1 to 15 October 2026, and offline on 3, 4, 10 and 11 October 2026. PWNSAT Prodigy (Level 2) is an offline exam at PW Vidyapeeth centres on 1 November 2026 for students shortlisted from Level 1 or recommended by their schools. Level 2 details are sent by WhatsApp/SMS to the registered mobile number and shown in the PW app.

## SC-3 · Exam pattern
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/vp-blogs/exams/what-is-pwnsat

Classes 5–10: 40 questions (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths and MAT/IQ). Classes 11–12 JEE: 30 questions (Physics, Chemistry, Maths). Classes 11–12 NEET: 60 questions (Physics, Chemistry, Biology). Prodigy: 60 minutes, +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one.

## SC-4 · Registering
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/vp-blogs/exams/what-is-pwnsat

Students register online through PW's official scholarship page (also reachable from the PW app) or at a PW centre, choose online or offline mode, and take the test on their chosen date.

## SC-5 · Using a scholarship
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/vp-blogs/exams/what-is-pwnsat and https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

The scholarship percentage depends on the student's PWNSAT result and applies to admission at PW Vidyapeeth centres. Documents (such as ID proof and marksheets) may be needed to confirm scholarship eligibility; centre staff help with this.

## SC-6 · PWNSAT fee
Source: official — https://www.pw.live/offline-centres/batches/vidyapeeth/centre-type/vidyapeeth/NEET-coaching/vidyapeeth-11th-neet--target-2027--975817

Any fee paid for PWNSAT is non-refundable.
