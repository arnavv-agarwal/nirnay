# Technical Support

## TS-1 · OTP not received
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

Check that the mobile number is entered correctly and the phone has network signal. Wait 60 seconds before tapping "Resend OTP". Do-not-disturb (DND) settings or a full SMS inbox can block OTPs. If no OTP arrives after 3 attempts, try again after 30 minutes. If it still fails, contact support with the registered number.

## TS-2 · Videos buffering or not playing
Source: assumed for this prototype; Low Data Mode is a public PW app feature

Lower the video quality from the player settings or switch on Low Data Mode, which uses much less data per lecture. Close other apps using the internet and update the PW app to the latest version. Downloading the lecture on Wi-Fi and watching it offline also avoids buffering.

## TS-3 · App crashing or not opening
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

Update the app to the latest version. On Android, clear the app's cache from Settings > Apps > Physics Wallah > Storage, then restart the phone. If it still crashes, reinstall the app; downloaded lectures may need to be downloaded again (TS-4).

## TS-4 · Offline downloads
Source: official — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.penpencil.physicswala ; limits assumed for this prototype

Lectures and notes can be downloaded in the PW app to study without internet. Downloads play only inside the PW app and only while the batch is valid. Reinstalling the app, clearing app storage, or logging in on another device removes downloads, and they must be downloaded again.

## TS-5 · Test or DPP not submitting
Source: assumed for this prototype (not published by PW)

Check the internet connection before submitting and don't close the app while a test submits. If a test was interrupted by a technical problem, the support team can check whether the attempt was recorded: share the test name, batch, and the date and time of the attempt.

## TS-6 · Logged out suddenly
Source: public reports of PW app behaviour — https://www.primebook.in/blog/how-to-use-physics-wallah-app-on-two-devices

Being logged out usually means the account was opened on another device (BA-3). Log in again with the registered mobile number. If the student did not log in elsewhere, they should tell support, because someone else may be using the account.
